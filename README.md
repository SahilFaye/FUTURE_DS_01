# FUTURE_DS_01: Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Project Overview
This repository contains Task 1 of the Data Science & Analytics Internship at Future Interns. The project analyzes multi-year Superstore business sales data to evaluate revenue trajectories, high-value product categories, customer segmentation, and regional operational performance using an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- *Total Sales*: $2.30M
- *Total Profit*: $286.40K
- *Top Category*: Technology
- *Primary Customer Segment*: Consumer (~50%)

---

## Dashboard Architecture

### 1. Overview Page
- *Executive KPI Cards*: Real-time aggregation of Total Sales and Total Profit.
- *Regional Performance Slicer*: Dynamic multi-region filtering (Central, East, South, West).
- *Sales by Category*: Horizontal distribution across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
- *Customer Segmentation*: Donut breakdown comparing Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office splits.
- *Monthly Revenue Trend*: Continuous 4-year time-series analysis highlighting end-of-year seasonal surges.

### 2. Product Analysis (Deep-Dive)
- *Top 10 Selling Products*: Top N filtered ranking isolating high-revenue inventory items.
- *Sub-Category Profitability*: Vertical margin distribution identifying net positive drivers vs. loss-making segments.

---

## Business Insights
1. *Seasonal Demand*: Revenue peaks consistently during Q4 (September–November), showing strong annual seasonality.
2. *Profitability Imbalance: While Technology and Copiers generate strong positive cash flows, **Tables* and *Bookcases* show negative margins despite reasonable sales volume.
3. *Core Volume Driver*: The Consumer segment generates approximately half of total business revenue.

---

## Strategic Recommendations
- *Discount Restructuring*: Re-evaluate discounting formulas on Tables and Bookcases to eliminate loss margins.
- *Proactive Inventory Planning*: Scale supply chain stocking for high-demand tech products ahead of the Q4 holiday surge.
- *Cross-Selling Strategy*: Implement targeted bundles of high-margin accessories with popular office hardware.

---

## Tools Used
- *Power BI Desktop*: Data modeling, DAX measures, dynamic filtering, modern card UI design.
- *Dataset*: Superstore Sales Data.
